# Case study: can a Polish app built for non-Slavic speakers keep an adult learner on a realistic plan?

Status: started 16 November 2026. Version 0.1.0 is built. There are no findings yet, only hypotheses.

## The question

I want to reach B2 in Polish. My first ask was B2 by the end of 2027 on 20 to 30 minutes a day. Does that add up, and can an app make the honest answer useful instead of discouraging?

## Approach

- Wrote 10 beginner lessons where every rule has a note comparing Polish with Greek, Italian or Spanish.
- Built a plan that counts hours studied, in the app and outside it, and turns pace into a date for each level.
- Added an AI tutor on the Claude API that corrects one main mistake per message in the learner's language.
- I am the first user. I will log my own study from [start date].

## Findings

### 1. The first goal did not fit the time (confirmed by arithmetic, estimate-based)

What: B2 on 25 minutes a day from mid November 2026 gives about 170 hours by the end of 2027. My planning estimate for B2 is about 800 hours.
Evidence: 410 days at 25 minutes. The 800 hour figure is my estimate, not an official one.
Why it matters: the product had to show this gap on day one. I changed my own goal to A2 first, and the Plan screen now shows the minutes a day any goal needs.

## What my own approach got wrong

- When I first set milestones I planned A2 by June 2027. The app's own numbers put A2 on 25 minutes a day in January 2028. The milestone was wrong and the tracker was right.
