# Product brief: Polski Daily

Version 0.1.0 · 16 November 2026

## Problem

Adults who move to Poland from countries with non-Slavic languages learn Polish slowly. Courses and apps explain grammar from an English point of view, or teach mixed groups where speakers of other Slavic languages set the pace. A Greek speaker already understands cases and three genders. An Italian or Spanish speaker does not, but knows verb endings that show the person. Generic material uses none of this.

A second problem is planning. Learners set goals such as "B2 next year" without knowing how many hours that takes. Streaks reward showing up, not progress toward a level.

## Users

- First user: me. Greek, living in Kraków, beginner, 20 to 30 minutes a day.
- Target users: adult speakers of Greek, Italian and Spanish living or working in Poland, studying alone in short daily sessions.
- Not the target: speakers of Slavic languages, children, and people preparing for the state exam next month.

## Goals for v0.1.0

1. A daily session that fits in 20 to 30 minutes on a phone.
2. Lessons that explain each Polish rule against the learner's own language.
3. A plan that shows, from logged time, when the learner will reach each level and what their goal needs per day.
4. A tutor conversation that gives corrections a beginner can understand.

## Scope

In: setup, daily session, 10 A1 lessons, review cards, AI tutor, plan and projections, backup.

Out for now: accounts and sync, audio and speaking, lessons past the start of A1, an interface in Greek, Italian or Spanish, payment.
